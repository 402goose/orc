# The ORC guild

The user's supplied ORC emblem is the canonical identity: pointed ears, a forehead
scar, a broad face and two ivory tusks. `orc-logo.png` is the original, unmodified
upload. The sidebar frames its visible content with an SVG viewBox; it does not
rewrite the source image.

The cast is Gruk the forgekeeper, Fusion the forge, Snout the truffle scout,
Laya the lorekeeper, the council (keepers of the seal), and the data garden.
The motto is **Big tusks. Small diffs. Show your work.** Their field guide is
available through **Meet the guild**. Lore decorates the product; workflow names,
statuses and operational instructions keep their ordinary meanings.

`../brand.js` contains 16 hand-authored SVG sigils: orc, forge, truffle, rune,
council, seed, compass, scroll, bug, shield, gear, library, spark, moon, sun and
flag. These inherit the active palette. `../brand.css` provides frames, rune rings,
sparkle motion and responsive layouts. Reduced-motion preferences disable motion.
Appearance settings tint the displayed raster artwork and regenerate the favicon;
the stored original assets remain unchanged. The logo's pale plaque preserves
the dark wordmark's contrast in light and dark modes.

## Companion artwork provenance

`forge-companions.png` was generated with the built-in imagegen tool, using the
user's logo as a character/style reference. It has a transparent background and
ships locally; neither image display nor icons require external requests.
